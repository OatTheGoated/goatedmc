💡 Adding YouTube Videos

To add YouTube videos to your page:

  1. Find a YouTube video you want to embed
  2. Get the video ID (the part after ?v=)
  3. Replace dQw4w9WgXcQ with your video ID in the iframe
  4. Save and refresh!
